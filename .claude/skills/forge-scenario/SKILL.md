---
name: forge-scenario
description: Convert verified job duties and licence competencies into a complete operator scenario.
---

Use verified vacancy IDs or a clearly labelled generic training family from `$ARGUMENTS`.

Create a data contract covering:
- full shift lifecycle;
- access/egress and inspection;
- site and machine configuration;
- controls and cameras;
- hazards and injected incidents;
- physics parameters;
- learning stages;
- scoring and causal debrief;
- simplifications and legal disclaimer.

Validate the result with `scripts/validate_scenario.py`.
